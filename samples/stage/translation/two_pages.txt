Northwind's local planning pilot

This fictional planning team will evaluate a small collection of synthetic
documents on a desktop workstation. The pilot covers meeting summaries,
briefing drafts, chart reading and multilingual communication. Its purpose is
to learn which tasks are useful and repeatable. It is not a deployment decision.

A human reviewer will compare each artifact with its source. The team will
record elapsed time, note missing information and keep original files intact.
The pilot owner will select the examples before the workshop. No real customer,
employee or patient records will be used.

The next discussion should agree on the scope, name the owner and choose the
evaluation checklist. Any performance or financial conclusion requires its own
measurement. Generated drafts are starting points for review.

Workshop and review

The workshop will demonstrate source reading, a short presentation, an email
draft and an original cover illustration. Participants will inspect which model
handled each task. Queued work must remain visible. A failed task must be reported
plainly so that a reviewer can decide what to do next.

The English draft will also be reviewed in Spanish and Japanese. Names, dates,
headings and page boundaries must be preserved. Translation does not imply that
a message has been sent or approved. The original document remains authoritative.

After review, the pilot owner will record the useful workflows and unresolved
questions. The team will then decide whether another technical session is
worthwhile. No purchase, staffing reduction or quantified benefit is promised.

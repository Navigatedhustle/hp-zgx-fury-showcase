## Action Trailer visitor data (owner-authorized V3 amendment)

Visitor photos are real personal data. Only Action Trailer's consented camera flow may collect a real visitor photo. The photo and resulting video are processed only on this machine through the local gateway and creative lane, in a per-session temporary directory outside the repository. Never log photos, image payloads, appearance descriptions or generated visitor media. Usage records may retain counts, model identity, routing and timing only. Never send visitor data to an external model or service; never include it in Git, source archives or screenshots committed to Git.

Consent must precede camera access and require a tap on “I am 18 or older, or my guardian approved, and I agree”. No consent means no camera. Action Trailer has no photo file-upload control. Require a secure browser context (localhost or the existing private HTTPS host); explain an unavailable camera without changing networking or Tailscale.

Delete the session's photo and video on Done, New visitor, explicit “Delete my photo and video now”, after 15 minutes idle, and on every showcase service start. Stop owned pending renders and discard their outputs when a session is deleted; never recreate a deleted session directory. Confirm deletion visibly. Acceptance must prove the temporary directory is empty after Done and after restart. Vision Lab and document samples use fictional/public demonstration data under the existing rules.

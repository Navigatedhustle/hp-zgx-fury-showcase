# Nano run of show

Five minutes of presenter pacing, with a hard maximum of seven minutes. “Measured” is live computation time; the presenter can hold a completed beat to finish the line. Measured times below are p50 and p95 from ten consecutive live native kiosk shows. Audio is off in the default show.

The comparison uses dated text token pricing only and is not total cost of ownership. The fictional notes, names, document and planning graphics are original demonstration data. The full email and deck remain private session artifacts; on screen the email is explicitly an excerpt.

Next: Right arrow, Page Down, Space or Enter. Back: Left arrow or Page Up. V toggles the current segment's backup. Hold R for two seconds to reset the segment. B or period toggles black. All other keys are ignored. Advances are debounced for 600 milliseconds.
